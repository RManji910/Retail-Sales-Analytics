# 📊 Retail Sales Analytics

Business Intelligence project using **PostgreSQL** to analyze retail sales data and generate actionable business insights.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Sample Superstore** dataset using PostgreSQL to answer real-world business questions about sales, profitability, customers, products, and geographic performance.

The objective is to demonstrate practical SQL skills while producing meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision making.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 📌 Revenue

- California generates the highest total sales.
- Technology is the highest revenue-generating category.
- Phones are the highest revenue-generating sub-category.

### 📌 Profitability

- California and New York are the most profitable states.
- Copiers generate the highest profit among all sub-categories.
- Furniture produces high sales but relatively low profit.

### 📌 Business Risks

- Texas is one of the highest revenue states but generates the largest overall loss.
- Tables are the least profitable sub-category despite producing significant sales.
- Some customers consistently generate negative profit, suggesting pricing or discounting opportunities.

### 📌 Business Growth

- Sales and profit increased consistently from 2014 through 2017.
- 2017 produced the highest sales and profit in the dataset.
